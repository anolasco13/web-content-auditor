About Lumen Bikes

We design electric bikes for people who would rather ride than sit in traffic.

Lumen started in 2024 with one goal: make a city e-bike that's light enough to carry up a flight of stairs.

Questions? Contact our team or read the W3C internationalization guides that shaped how we build our site.
